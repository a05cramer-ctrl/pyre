How it works

Four steps. No sign-up for anyone.

01

Pick a trader

Every Phoenix Perpetuals account is public onchain. Browse the board or paste any Solana wallet that trades there.

02

Launch the coin

Name it, pick a ticker, drop an image, then create it on pump.fun with creator-fee sharing pointed at that trader's wallet.

03

People trade it

It's a normal pump.fun coin: bonding curve first, then the open market. Every buy and sell generates pump.fun creator fees.

04

The trader gets paid

pump.fun's creator-fee sharing sends 90% to the trader's wallet and 10% to the PYRE treasury. They never sign up and never claim.

The split

Creator fees, split 90/10.Most of it goes to the trader.

Every trade on the coin pays pump.fun's creator fee. pump.fun's own creator-fee sharing routes it on every trade: 90% to the trader you picked, 10% to the PYRE treasury. No vault, no claim button, no middleman wallet.
